POSTDOCTORAL RESEARCHER · PEKING UNIVERSITY

Yuan Yao.

From quantum light
to fault-tolerant computation.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Peking University, working with Prof. Xiao Yuan. I received my Ph.D. from Télécom Paris in 2023 under the supervision of Filippo M. Miatto and Nicolás Quesada. Before joining Peking University, I worked as a Quantum Architecture Scientist at Xanadu.

My research spans fault-tolerant quantum computing, quantum error correction, variational quantum algorithms, and the simulation and automated design of photonic quantum circuits.
